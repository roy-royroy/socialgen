GOOD QUESTIONS.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Everything to know before your first burn.

What do I receive?

A Social Bundle containing one email account and associated X / Twitter, TikTok, and Instagram accounts.

How much does it cost?

The configured $SOCIALGEN burn amount plus a separate fixed 0.05 SOL service fee. The token burn amount will be announced at launch. Solana network fees are additional.

Are the tokens actually burned?

Once the production blockchain integration is enabled, the required $SOCIALGEN will be permanently burned and the transaction will be publicly verifiable on Solana. Burns are not enabled during pre-launch.

Why is there a 0.05 SOL fee?

The 0.05 SOL is a service/redemption fee. It is separate from the $SOCIALGEN token burn.

Can I change the usernames?

Username changes depend on availability and the rules of each respective platform. All accounts and their use must comply with those platform rules.

When do I receive my bundle?

After the required blockchain transactions and provisioning have been successfully confirmed. Redemptions are not yet open.

What blockchain is $SOCIALGEN on?

Solana.
